## 2. Golden Shine Cultural Welfare Trust – Certificate of Outstanding Service
**Organization:** Golden Shine Cultural Welfare Trust  
**Event:** Spark Inclusive Carnival 2024  
**Venue:** Dr. Ambedkar International Centre, New Delhi  
**Date:** 23 June 2024  

I received a **Certificate of Outstanding Service** for volunteering and supporting the successful execution of the Spark Inclusive Carnival 2024.

The event focused on:
- Inclusivity and accessibility
- Support for differently-abled individuals
- Community empowerment and engagement

### Volunteer Contributions
- Assisted visually impaired attendees during the event
- Coordinated with volunteers and organizers
- Supported on-ground event execution
- Managed attendee guidance and engagement activities

The event supported **500+ visually impaired attendees**, providing valuable exposure to teamwork, communication, and community-focused operations.
